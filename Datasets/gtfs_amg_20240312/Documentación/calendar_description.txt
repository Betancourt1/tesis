calendar.csv is a CSV file that contains information about the service schedules in a transit system. The file has ten columns:

1. service_id: A unique identifier for each service schedule.
2. monday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Mondays.
3. tuesday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Tuesdays.
4. wednesday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Wednesdays.
5. thursday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Thursdays.
6. friday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Fridays.
7. saturday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Saturdays.
8. sunday: A binary indicator (0 or 1) specifying whether the service operates on Sundays.
9. start_date: The start date for the service schedule in the format YYYYMMDD.
10. end_date: The end date for the service schedule in the format YYYYMMDD.

Each row represents a specific service schedule, detailing the days of the week it operates and the period during which it is active.
